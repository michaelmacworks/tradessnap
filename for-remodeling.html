Remodeler FAQ

Questions we hear from remodelers like you.

My projects take weeks. When do I record the memo? +

Right at the reveal, when you hand the keys back to the homeowner. That's when the story is freshest and most compelling. The transformation is complete, the homeowner is happiest, and you have all the details fresh in your mind. Record for 60–90 seconds, upload one or two photos, and you're done.

What if I don't have a professional photographer? +

Phone photos of a great remodel beat professional photos of a mediocre one every time. Authentic and local wins. The contrast between before and after is what matters — pull the dated cabinet photo from your initial walkthrough, snap the new space with your phone, and you're set. Your homeowner's reaction is more powerful than any staging.

I work mostly on kitchens and baths. Is that enough content? +

Absolutely. Two quality remodel posts per month consistently beats most competitors who post nothing. Focus and depth matter more than variety. When homeowners search "kitchen remodeler in [your city]," they'll find your case studies before they find the competition.

Can it handle design-heavy language like finishes and fixtures? +

Yes. TradesSnap.us translates specifications like "3x6 subway tile with pencil liner trim" into language that excites homeowners without losing the credibility. It knows the difference between marketing speak and craft. Your voice — and your standards — come through.

How long until I see inquiries from new neighborhoods? +

Most remodelers see Google Business Profile view increases within 2–4 weeks of consistent posting. New neighborhood inquiries typically start within 60–90 days. The key is consistency — documenting jobs every week compounds over time into a permanent local reputation that's very hard for competitors to replicate quickly.
