Contractor FAQ

Questions we hear from contractors like you.

My projects take weeks or months. When do I record the voice memo? +

Record a quick walkthrough at completion. Even a 90-second summary at handoff is all you need. "Just finished a basement in Blue Ash — framed it out, wired it, hung drywall, LVP throughout, wet bar in the corner. Homeowner loved it." That's enough. TradesSnap.us fills in the scope.

My jobs involve multiple trades. Will the AI handle that? +

Yes. TradesSnap.us understands multi-trade scopes and writes about each component in homeowner-friendly language. A basement finish with framing, electrical, drywall, and flooring becomes a complete case study where each trade is explained as homeowner value, not jargon.

I work mostly on referrals. Do I still need SEO? +

Referrals dry up. Local SEO is your insurance policy — and the best GCs use both. The ones who document their work online have steady inquiry flow even in slow referral months. You're protecting your pipeline.

What if the project photos aren't professional quality? +

Job site photos taken on a phone are exactly what homeowners trust. Authentic beats polished every time. A raw basement before, a finished space after — shot on a jobsite phone — converts better than a stock photo ever will.

How long until I see new inquiries from Google? +

Most contractors see Google Business Profile view increases within 2-4 weeks. Website ranking improvements follow in 60-90 days of consistent posting. The key is consistency — documenting jobs every week compounds over time into a permanent SEO advantage that's very hard for competitors to replicate quickly.
