Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

At TradesSnap.us, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

1. Who We Are

TradesSnap.us is operated by TradesSnap.us, LLC. We provide a SaaS platform that helps home service contractors turn voice memos and job photos into marketing content. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at hello@tradessnap.us.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in several ways to provide and improve our service:

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and the type of trade or service you provide.
  • Voice Memos: Audio recordings you submit to generate content. These are processed by AI and deleted from our servers within 30 days of submission.
  • Job Photos: Images you upload to generate labeled Before/After assets. These are stored only as long as the content exists in your dashboard.
  • Usage Data: We collect standard analytics about how you use the platform—which pages you visit, which features you use—to help us improve the product.
  • Payment Information: Payment details are processed by Stripe, our payment provider. We never store full credit card numbers on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To generate your marketing content (converting voice memos to transcripts, then to finished outputs)
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the TradesSnap.us service
  • To send you important product updates and account notices (not marketing spam)
  • To respond to your support requests and inquiries

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes. We do not use your content to train or improve AI models without your explicit consent.

4. Your Voice Memos and Photos

Voice Memos: When you submit a voice memo, we transcribe it using AI technology. The transcript is then used to generate your marketing content. The raw audio file is automatically deleted from our servers within 30 days of submission.

Job Photos: Your photos are used to generate labeled Before/After images for your marketing assets. We do not alter, filter, or enhance your photos beyond adding text labels. You retain full ownership of your original images.

Content Ownership: Your voice memos, photos, and all marketing content we generate for you belong to you. We claim no ownership over your media or the outputs we create from them.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on encrypted servers located in the United States. We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your information, including encryption in transit and at rest, secure authentication, and regular security reviews.

However, no system is 100% secure. If a security breach occurs that compromises your personal information, we will notify you promptly by email and provide details about the incident and any steps you should take.

6. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party providers to deliver our service:

  • Stripe: Payment processing
  • AI Services: Transcription and content generation (your content is not used to train AI models)
  • Analytics: Standard web analytics tools to understand how the platform is used

We want to be clear: we do not integrate with Google, Facebook, Instagram, or any social platform on your behalf. You copy-paste the marketing content we generate into those platforms yourself. No API connections are made to any social network without your explicit action.

7. Data Retention

While Your Account Is Active: All account data is retained as long as your subscription is active.

After Cancellation: If you cancel your subscription, we will delete your account data within 90 days of your request.

Voice Memos: Voice memos are deleted within 30 days of submission, regardless of whether your account is active.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we have about you
  • Correct any inaccurate information
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Cancel your subscription anytime from your dashboard with no penalty

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact us at hello@tradessnap.us and we'll respond within 10 business days.

9. Cookies

We use minimal cookies to keep you logged in and to collect basic, non-identifying analytics about platform usage. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising, retargeting, or third-party analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may limit your ability to use certain features of the platform.

10. Children's Privacy

TradesSnap.us is intended for business use by adults and is not directed toward anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from someone under 18, we will delete it immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Your continued use of TradesSnap.us after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
