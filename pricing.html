Pricing Questions

The answers you're
looking for.

Do I have to sign a contract? +

No contracts, ever. Month-to-month subscription. Cancel anytime from your dashboard — no cancellation fees, no questions asked, no runaround.

What counts as a job post? +

Each voice memo submission counts as one post. One submission generates all three outputs — blog, Google Business, and social — and uses one of your 30 monthly posts. At $97/month that's about $3.23 per complete content set. Most contractors would spend 30–60 minutes writing just one of these manually.

What happens if I hit my 30-post limit? +

You'll get a notification when you're getting close. When you hit the limit, you wait until your next billing cycle resets. No overage charges, no surprise fees. If you're regularly maxing out and want more, reach out — we'll work something out.

Do I need to connect any accounts or platforms? +

Nothing. No Google API, no Instagram login, no WordPress plugin. You sign up, set your trade and location, and start recording. When your content is ready, you copy it from your dashboard and paste it wherever you want. You stay in full control of your own platforms.

What if I want to try it before committing? +

Sign up, document your first job, and see the output for yourself. If it's not worth every dollar before your next billing date, cancel. That's the only guarantee we need to offer — the work speaks for itself.

Will the price ever go up? +

We'll always be upfront if anything changes. Early customers lock in the rate they signed up at for as long as they stay subscribed. If we ever adjust pricing, existing subscribers keep their current plan.
