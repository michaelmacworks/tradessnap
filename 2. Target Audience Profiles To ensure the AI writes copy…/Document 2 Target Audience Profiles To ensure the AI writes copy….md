**Document 2: Target Audience Profiles** To ensure the AI writes copy that actually converts, it must understand the two distinct "people" it is talking to: the **Searcher** (Homeowner) and the **Algorithm** (Google).** 1. The Primary Audience: The "Stressed Homeowner"** This person isn't reading for leisure; they are reading to solve a problem. They are likely on a mobile device, potentially in a state of mild panic or frustration. • **Psychographic Profile:** Values reliability, speed, and local proximity. They want to know, "Have you done this exact job for someone in my neighborhood?" • **Pain Points:** * Hidden costs or "salesy" language. ◦ Technical jargon they don't understand (e.g., "What is a heat exchanger?"). ◦ Fear of hiring a "fly-by-night" contractor who won't show up. • **The "Hook":** Seeing a photo and a description of a house that looks just like theirs, located just five minutes away.** 2. The Secondary Audience: The Google Local Algorithm** While not a person, the AI must treat the algorithm as an audience member that requires specific "food" to rank the contractor. • **Search Intent:** Matches "Service + City" or "Problem + Near Me" queries. • **Relevance Signals:** Needs to see consistent mentions of specific landmarks, neighborhoods, and suburbs (e.g., "Replacing a sump pump in **North Linden**" vs. just "Columbus"). • **Freshness:** Google favors businesses that post frequent, unique updates (GBPs and Blogs) over static websites.** 3.
