I do a lot of maintenance work, not just big installs. Will this work for mowing and cleanup jobs? +

Absolutely. Even a routine spring cleanup or leaf removal job generates useful local content — "Seasonal Cleanup in Maplewood Estates" still ranks for neighbors searching "yard cleanup service near me." Every job, big or small, builds your local authority over time. Volume is your advantage.

I work in multiple cities and zip codes. Can TradesSnap handle that? +

Yes — that's actually your superpower. Every job gets geo-tagged to its specific neighborhood and city. A roofer in three cities gets three times the local content. Just mention the location in your voice memo and we handle the rest.

How many photos can I upload per job? +

Up to 10 photos per job. Our AI selects the best before-and-after pairs for the slider, and uses the others for the blog post and social captions. More photos = more compelling content. We recommend at least one wide before, one wide after, and one or two detail shots.

Do I need a website for this to work? +

No website needed to start. Google Business Profile and Instagram work independently from day one. Your GBP posts drive local search visibility immediately. When you do add a website, all the blog content is ready to import — your SEO foundation is already built.

I'm a solo operator, not a big crew. Is this worth it for me? +

Solo operators benefit most. You're doing 3-6 jobs a week — that's 3-6 pieces of local content with zero extra effort.
