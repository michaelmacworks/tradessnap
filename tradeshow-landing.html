FAQ

Questions from real contractors.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use this? +

Not even a little. If you can record a voice memo on your phone and take a photo, you can use TradesSnap.us. The whole system was designed around contractors who are on the go, not at a desk. Setup takes under 10 minutes and we walk you through every step.

Will the AI make stuff up about my jobs? +

No — this is core to how we built the system. TradesSnap.us is instructed to never invent pricing, brand names, or customer details. If a detail isn't in your voice note, the AI uses accurate industry-standard language instead. Your reputation stays intact.

What if I don't have a website yet? +

You can still use the Google Business Profile and Social Media outputs right away — those don't require a website. When you're ready to add a site, we support WordPress and Webflow with one-click setup. We can point you toward simple, affordable options if you need them.

How long does content generation take? +

Usually under 90 seconds from the time you submit your voice memo and photos. You'll get a push notification when everything is ready. Most contractors have all three outputs reviewed and posted before they've finished loading the tools back in the truck.

Will it actually sound like me, not a robot? +

That's the whole point. TradesSnap.us is built around the "Blue-Collar Authority" voice — direct, local, proud of the craft, and zero corporate buzzwords. Every output sounds the way a trusted local pro actually talks. You review and tweak anything before it publishes.

How soon will I see Google results? +

Most contractors start seeing Google Business Profile view increases within the first 2–4 weeks of consistent posting. Organic website ranking improvements typically follow over 60–90 days. The more jobs you document, the faster Google recognizes you as an active, authoritative local business.
