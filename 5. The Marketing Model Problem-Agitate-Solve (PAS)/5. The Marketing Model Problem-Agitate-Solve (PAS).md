5. The Marketing Model: "Problem-Agitate-Solve" (PAS)  
For home services, the most effective marketing model is **PAS**. It works because homeowners usually find you when they are annoyed or scared. • **Problem:** Identify the issue (e.g., "Water is leaking through the ceiling"). • **Agitate:** Highlight the risk of ignoring it (e.g., "Mold growth and structural damage can cost thousands if left alone"). • **Solve:** Present the contractor as the hero (e.g., "Summit Ridge Roofing provides 24-hour emergency tarping"). **Instruction for Claude:** *"In every Case Study, use the PAS framework. Ensure the 'Problem' section feels urgent and the 'Solution' feels like a massive relief."*** 2. The SEO Keyword Strategy (Semantic Clustering)** To rank for "Plumber in [City]," you can't just say "Plumber." You need to mention **Semantic Keywords**—words Google expects to see near the main topic.**Main ServiceSemantic Keywords to IncludeRoofing**Decking, flashing, drip edge, ice and water shield, shingles.**Plumbing**PEX, PVC, main line, snake, pressure valve, fixtures.**HVAC**Condenser, refrigerant, thermostat, airflow, furnace. **Instruction for Claude:** *"When a trade is identified, naturally weave in 2-3 technical 'parts' or 'components' related to that trade to boost topical authority for Google."*** 3. Data Schema Instructions (For the Website)** If you want this website to be truly "automated" and high-ranking, the AI needs to output **JSON-LD Schema**. This is invisible code that tells Google: *"This is a Local Business, and this post is a real job we did at these coordinates."* **Instruction for Claude:** *"In addition to the three text deliverables, generate a simple JSON-LD 'CaseStudy' schema block including the Business Name, Service, and Location."*** 4. The "Negative Constraints" (What NOT to do)** To keep the brand high-end, give Claude a list of "Icks" to avoid: • **No Puns:** Avoid "We're the 'top' choice for roofing!" (It sounds cheesy). • **No Over-promising:** Never say "We guarantee the lowest price" unless it's in the transcript. • **No Emoji Overload:** Keep it to 3 max per social post.
