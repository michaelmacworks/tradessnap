Plumbing · Columbus, OH

From zero Google presence to
booking 2 weeks out.

A solo plumber with no social presence and a stagnant Google profile turned consistent TradesSnap use into a wait list.

+312%
Profile views
in 90 days
The Challenge

Running a solo operation, Marcus had no time to write content. His Google Business profile had two photos from 2021 and hadn't been updated since. Competitors were ranking above him despite doing inferior work.

How He Used TradesSnap

After each job, Marcus recorded a 60-second voice memo describing the work — what broke, what he fixed, where the job was. He uploaded a before/after photo pair and hit submit. The whole process took under two minutes.

The Results

After 12 weeks of posting 3–4 times per week, Google profile views were up 312%. Monthly calls from Google jumped from 4 to 17. By week 14, he was booking two weeks out for the first time in his business.

"I always knew I needed to do marketing. I just never had the time or knew what to say. This thing does the writing for me — I just talk about my job." — Marcus T., Solo Plumber, Columbus OH
HVAC · Phoenix, AZ

The Challenge

Valley Cool Air had a basic website and decent reviews, but weren't showing up for "AC repair Phoenix" or any neighborhood-level searches. Their SEO agency wanted $2,500/month — way out of budget for a 4-person shop.

How They Used TradesSnap

The owner had technicians submit a voice memo and photos at the end of every AC repair and installation. Each submission generated a geo-tagged SEO blog post that went straight to their WordPress site. No editor needed.

The Results

Within 60 days, 6 blog posts were ranking on page 1 for hyper-local queries like "AC repair Scottsdale" and "emergency HVAC Chandler AZ." Monthly website visitors doubled. Cost: $97/month instead of $2,500.

"We were paying an SEO company $2,500 a month and barely moving. TradesSnap costs $97 and we're on page one for six terms in under two months. The math is ridiculous." — Diana R., Owner, Valley Cool Air

The Challenge

Ridge Runner Roofing had hired a social media manager twice and quit twice. The content always felt generic, cost too much to maintain, and generated zero leads. The owner was convinced social media didn't work for roofing.

How They Used TradesSnap

The crew lead started submitting a memo after every significant job — storm damage repairs, full replacements, gutters. The before/after photos with labeled badges became their standard Instagram format: real work, real results.

The Results

Within 3 months, Instagram followers grew from 140 to 890. Two to three inbound DM leads per month, with 2 converting to booked jobs at an average ticket of $8,400. Monthly ROI on TradesSnap: over 100x.

"I told my wife social media was a waste of money. I take it back. The difference is the content is about our actual jobs — not generic roofing tips some kid in an office made up." — Jeff K., Owner, Ridge Runner Roofing
